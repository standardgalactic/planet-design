# Terminal Desolation

A lone courier uncovers a forgotten past when delivering to an obsolete station, navigating the haunting beauty of a salt desert terminal.

In the desolate expanse of a 1970s science fiction world, a solitary figure navigates through monumental concrete architecture bathed in warm amber light. The setting is a now-abandoned spaceport terminal, its once-vibrant neon signs faded to muted earth tones. Amidst the sprawling salt desert landscape, the courier stands before an ancient structure, its glass walls reflecting the barren surroundings.
