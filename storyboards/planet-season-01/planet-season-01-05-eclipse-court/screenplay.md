# Shadow's Embrace

A caretaker uncovers an ancient observatory hidden within the shadows of a ceremonial court during a rare eclipse.

In the heart of a 1970s science fiction world, where monumental concrete architecture stands under muted earth tones and warm amber light, a solitary caretaker tends to an empty ceremonial court. The scene opens with the caretaker preparing for the upcoming eclipse, ensuring every detail is perfect for the celestial event that will unveil hidden secrets. As the night falls, the sky darkens, revealing the court bathed in shadows. A single shot captures the sweeping view of the court, its glass walls reflecting the stars above, emphasizing the anticipation of the unfolding mystery.
