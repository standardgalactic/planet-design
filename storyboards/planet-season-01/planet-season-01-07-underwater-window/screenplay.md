# Submerged Sentinel

An observatory technician uncovers a stranded maintenance vessel after noticing an unusual light moving outside the submerged window, leading to its rescue.


INT. OBSERVATORY - NIGHT

A lone TECHNICIAN (late 20s, glasses, uniform) stands by a large SUBMERGED WINDOW overlooking a vast oceanic expanse beneath the observatory. The room is dimly lit with warm amber lights from old fluorescent tubes. The technician fiddles with a small handheld scanner, occasionally glancing at the moving light outside.

1. Establishing View - Duration: 15 seconds
   Action: Wide shot of the observatory interior and the endless blue expanse beyond the window. The technician is visible in silhouette against the glow of the scanner.
   Camera: Static crane shot.
   Visual Prompt: Silhouette of TECHNICIAN holding a handheld scanner, with the glowing light outside the glass reflecting on the concrete walls.

INT. OBSERVATORY - NIGHT (CONT'D)

The technician finally identifies the pattern of the moving light as consistent and deliberate, indicating movement beyond the window.

2. Medium Composition - Duration: 15 seconds
   Action: Close-up on the technician's face, eyes wide with curiosity, hands steady as they adjust the scanner to follow the light's path.
   Camera: Handheld shot from above, focusing on TECHNICIAN's expression and movements.
   Visual Prompt: Technician's focused face illuminated by scanner glow, hands moving in rhythm with the movement of the light outside.

INT. OBSERVATORY - NIGHT (CONT'D)

Realizing the potential for a stranded vessel, the technician decides to investigate further despite the risks.

3. Discovery - Duration: 15 seconds
   Action: Medium shot showing the technician leaving their station and walking towards the window with the scanner in hand.
   Camera: Tracking shot following TECHNICIAN's movement toward the window.
   Visual Prompt: Technician moving through shadowed corridors, light from the scanner casting long shadows on the concrete floor.

EXT. UNDERWATER - NIGHT (IMAGINED)

The technician imagines what lies beneath the surface where the light is coming from, a sense of urgency growing.

4. Consequence - Duration: 15 seconds
   Action: Wide shot of an imagined underwater scene with dark water and distant glow representing potential shipwreck or debris.
   Camera: Stillscape with atmospheric fog enhancing mystery.
   Visual Prompt: Dark oceanic backdrop with faint, ghostly outlines hinting at a submerged object emitting the light seen through the window.

INT. OBSERVATORY - NIGHT (CONT'D)

The technician contacts the maintenance crew via intercom to alert them of the possible stranded vessel.

5. Resolution Setup - Duration: 15 seconds
   Action: Medium shot of TECHNICIAN on the intercom, speaking clearly into a microphone.
   Camera: Close-up focusing on TECHNICIAN's hands and mouth as they speak.
   Visual Prompt: Technician visible through reflection in glass with words "Possible stranded vessel" echoing across the room.

INT. MAINTENANCE DEPARTMENT - NIGHT (IMAGINED)

The crew mobilizes, preparing for a rescue operation based on the technician’s information.

6. Rescue Preparation - Duration: 15 seconds
   Action: Wide shot of the maintenance department area with various equipment visible and workers in protective gear moving around.
   Camera: Static shot capturing activity and readiness.
   Visual Prompt: Workers assembling salvage tools near a large hatch, illuminated by warm lighting indicative of urgency.

EXT. UNDERWATER - NIGHT (IMAGINED)

The maintenance vessel is located using the information provided by the observatory technician, rescuing those on board.

7. Rescue Execution - Duration: 15 seconds
   Action: Medium shot of divers in suits emerging from a submersible onto an underwater scene with the stranded vessel nearby.
   Camera: Follow-the-action shot focusing on diver movements and the proximity to the vessel.
   Visual Prompt: Divers silhouetted against the dark water, light from their helmets casting patterns on the hull of the stranded vessel.

INT. OBSERVATORY - NIGHT (FINAL)

The technician watches through the window as the rescue operation concludes successfully with all personnel safe.

8. Resolved Ending - Duration: 15 seconds
   Action: Wide shot of TECHNICIAN back in the observatory, hands on scanner, looking relieved and satisfied.
   Camera: Static establishing shot showing the entire room calm after the event.
   Visual Prompt: Technician standing with a sense of accomplishment, light from outside window now steady as the operation is complete.
