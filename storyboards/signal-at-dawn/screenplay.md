# Signal of the Botanical Station

A maintenance worker uncovers an abandoned botanical station still broadcasting a mysterious signal, leading to unexpected revelations in a quiet coastal city.

In the early morning light, a solitary figure in rugged overalls and a worn hat stands at the edge of an abandoned botanical station. The concrete walls are covered with moss and vines, hinting at its forgotten purpose. As the worker's binoculars scan the sky, a faint signal is detected emanating from the station. Curious, they approach cautiously, their shadow stretching long beside them. Inside, the air is still and humid, with remnants of plant life scattered across the floor. The maintenance worker discovers an old logbook detailing experiments that never reached fruition, accompanied by sketches of unknown flora. As dawn breaks, the signal's strength grows, drawing a crowd of curious onlookers who gather at the station's entrance. Among them is a local historian dressed in 1970s attire, both characters sharing a quiet conversation about the significance of the signal and its potential to unlock secrets of the past. The mystery unfolds as they explore the station together, leading to a resolution where the true purpose behind the signal is revealed through old photographs and notes, leaving viewers with a sense of wonder and connection to history. The scene ends softly at dusk, the city waking up around them, the light filtering through curved glass windows casting warm amber hues across the environment.
