# The Last Projectionist: Scene Outline

Catalogue slot 03.01 | 18 scenes in three acts | Three locations: auditorium, projection booth, Pell's Café (cellar as a subsidiary space of the café)

## Governing rules

- **1987 appears only as attributed reconstruction.** Every image of the festival night is drawn from a named source (Aurel's drawing, Benedikt's telling, Tobias's account, Daniel's letter) and carries the shared reconstruction marker: perforation-edged frames with a flickering inner border. The marker never signals which account is right.
- **The one exception is Scene 18**, where the supplementary passage appears as the actual screen in the actual auditorium. It confirms what was projected. It is not an omniscient flashback to Daniel's departure.
- **Two separate inverse problems run in parallel.**
  - *Problem A, the screening history.* H1 cover-up cut at Harlan Voss's order. H2 conflated memory. H3 source censorship before delivery. H4 one-night insert. H5 physical loss.
  - *Problem B, Daniel's departure.* D1 left voluntarily with help. D2 harmed by someone connected to the Voss family. D3 left alone, circumstances unknown.
  - Aurel's central error is binding A and B into one explanation. His correction is to separate them and weigh the evidence for each independently.
- **Timing fact.** 41 feet of 35 mm at 24 fps runs about 27 seconds. The nine minutes is the festival's scheduled intermission, during which the insert was projected. The logbook records that the house lights were lowered again for a supplementary projection. The return to the feature is the second additional changeover.
- **Aurel's arrival.** Thirty-eight years of service puts him in the booth from about 1988, the season after the disputed festival. He inherited the logbook and has handled its pages for his whole career without reading that entry as evidence.
- **Structural device.** Priya's inventory count runs down with the deadline. Each scene opens on a day number and a count of items still unassigned (screened, transferred, retained, disposed). The count reaches zero in Scene 18.

What each observation does:

- O1 fits H1, H3, H4 and strains H2 and H5. It does not identify who "H.V." is.
- O2 fits H1, H4, H5. It does not date the memory or fix its source.
- O3 shows the delivered print lacked the footage, subject to the label's provenance. It eliminates H1 and H5 for the delivered print. It does not rule out a later insert and removal.
- O4 shows a separate projection and narrows toward H4. It does not say what was projected.
- O5 with O6 and O7 makes H4 strongly supported through conjunction. For Problem B, the letter plus two participants' accounts support D1. The screening records give chronology, not intention.

## Act One: An explanation takes shape

**Scene 01. The last inventory.** Booth, morning. Day 9.
Aurel prepares the projectors with practiced precision. Priya sets out the inventory procedure. He knows where everything belongs; she asks how he knows which version of each film an object represents. They disagree practically: should a repaired print be described only by its title? The deadline arrives through the work itself. Everything must be screened, transferred, retained or disposed of before the building is surrendered. Aurel treats the inventory as an intrusion on his last week.
*Live:* nothing yet. *Visual:* the count card, the booth in hard charcoal, no reconstruction marker anywhere.

**Scene 02. Two hands on the leader.** Booth, afternoon.
Rewinding *The Salt Orchard*, Aurel finds O1. Two other prints carry similar notes. He recognizes Edmund's hand but not the second. He reads "H.V." aloud as "Harlan Voss." Priya transcribes it exactly and leaves the expansion blank. First small difference between their methods.
*Live:* H1, H3, H4. *Visual:* the leader as a flat observed object, the two hands rendered distinguishably.

**Scene 03. The scene Benedikt remembers.** Café, evening.
Benedikt describes the red van, the lake-road turn, the cello phrase and a disturbance in the audience. He places it at the 1987 harvest festival unprompted. Aurel begins with open questions, then introduces the idea of a removal. Benedikt responds to the suggestion, but the scene preserves which details came before it. Margo recognizes the evening and steers talk to the closing programme.
*Live:* H1, H3, H4, H5. *Visual:* first reconstruction frames appear, attributed to Benedikt only.

**Scene 04. A missing passage.** Auditorium and booth, next morning. Day 8.
Aurel and Priya examine the surviving print. The passage is absent. The omission matches the footage count, but the print cannot say when it happened. Priya agrees an insertion is technically possible at the indicated position. Aurel hears confirmation of his historical account.
*Live:* unchanged. *Visual:* adjacent-frame assembly drawn as observed material, hard-edged.

**Scene 05. The first reconstruction.** Booth, evening.
Aurel draws the imagined sequence: festival screening, an instruction from Harlan, a cut afterward. Marked frames enter only as his drawing and narration develop. He then adds Daniel's disappearance. No new observation connects the two events, yet the account gains motive and force exactly where its support is weakest. This is the binding of Problems A and B.
*Live:* Aurel treats H1 and D2 as one account. *Visual:* the marker frames take over the page gradually, then recede to the drawing board.

**Scene 06. The owner's son.** Auditorium. Day 7.
Tobias arrives about the building handover. Aurel mentions the festival and the van. Tobias's manner changes and he refuses to discuss Daniel. Aurel reads distress and refusal as corroboration. Tobias asks whether the final screening will be used for accusations. The immediate threat to the closing event is set.
*Live:* silence is logged as a missing observation by Priya, as a finding by Aurel.

## Act Two: Coherence becomes accusation

**Scene 07. Rehearsing the memory.** Café. Day 6.
Aurel brings his drawings to Benedikt. Benedikt recognizes some elements and starts accommodating others, including who stood near the aisle. Margo points out he could not have seen that position from his seat. Aurel treats this as a minor correction to an established account. The reconstruction is reshaping its own testimony.
*Visual:* Benedikt's reconstruction frames now contain details taken from Aurel's drawings; the marker is identical.

**Scene 08. The overlooked surfaces.** Booth, afternoon.
Priya photographs cans, leaders, splices and labels as separate objects. Aurel works on assembling the missing passage and treats the containers as peripheral. The lab label enters plainly, with no dramatic close-up. Priya notices its date and asks for the delivery register, which Aurel fetches without examining the relation between them.
*Visual:* the label sits in a wide panel among other cans.

**Scene 09. The public claim.** Café. Day 5.
With the principals present, Aurel states his reconstruction as established history. He says Harlan ordered the removal and implies Tobias knows what happened to Daniel. Tobias demands the line between evidence and accusation. Aurel answers with the notes, the memory and the silence, treating compatibility as exclusivity. Tobias leaves. Margo's failure to contradict becomes, in Aurel's telling, another confirmation.
*Live in Aurel's mind:* one account. *Live on the page:* H1, H3, H4 for A; D1, D2, D3 for B.

**Scene 10. The cost arrives.** Auditorium, next morning.
Tobias says the closing screening cannot proceed as proposed. He does not threaten the records or the reel; his objection is to public use of the event. Priya asks which observation specifically establishes a Voss instruction. Aurel repeats his reading of the initials. She places the exact inscription beside the interpretation and the gap shows.

**Scene 11. Delivered short.** Booth. Day 4.
Priya sets the lab label against the dated delivery register. Together they establish that the Meridian received a print already missing the passage. H1 fails for the delivered print. Aurel does not reopen the hypothesis set. He swaps in source censorship plus mistaken recollection and explains Benedikt's memory through trailers or publicity. The second account is held as firmly as the first.
*Live:* H3, H4 (H2 returns in Aurel's version). *Visual:* second reconstruction uses the same marker as the first.

**Scene 12. A second certainty.** Café, evening.
Aurel tells Benedikt the screening could not have happened as described. Benedikt, unsettled by the earlier rehearsals, cannot separate his own recollection from Aurel's drawings. Margo objects that Aurel has gone from explaining the memory too confidently to dismissing it too confidently. He asks for her account. She refuses to break Daniel's confidence but recognizes her silence is now harming people.

## Act Three: The records separate

**Scene 13. Two changes too many.** Booth. Day 2.
Priya reconciles the programme, reel count and logbook. She finds two additional changeovers inside the scheduled nine-minute intermission, with a short note recording the supplementary projection and the return to the feature. The entry supports a separate projection. It does not say the insert held the deleted scene. Aurel starts to supply that identification, stops, and writes it as a question. First visible change in his practice.
*Live:* H4 gains ground; H1, H2, H3 strained. *Visual:* the logbook page as an observed object, the 41 ft / 27 s arithmetic shown on Priya's pad.

**Scene 14. An apology without a completed explanation.** Auditorium, later that day.
Aurel asks Tobias to meet and withdraws the accusation. He states what the records establish and what is unknown, without substituting a new verdict. Tobias confirms he drove Daniel to the bus stop and describes the departure as voluntary. Aurel asks permission to record the account.
*Problem B:* D1 now has a first-hand account; it is not yet corroborated.

**Scene 15. What the promise protected.** Café, evening.
Margo acknowledges her part. She explains Daniel's fear of going home and the letter she later received. Tobias and Margo have to face why the promise lasted after its protective reasons had changed. The scene keeps the difficulty and does not turn the promise into a clean vindication. Margo brings the letter and reel up from the cellar.

**Scene 16. Converging records.** Booth, final morning. Day 1.
Aurel and Priya check the reel against the leader instruction, footage count and Benedikt's original description. It contains the red-van passage and the cello phrase (O5). The lake road in the footage is identifiable and matches the road Tobias drove (O7). Daniel's letter describes the supplementary screening and his departure; its date and provenance are recorded. The second hand on the leaders matches the letter. The programme or a technical note expands "H.V." as Harvest Viewing (O6), found rather than asserted.
*Result:* Problem A: H4 strongly supported. Problem B: D1 rests on the letter and two accounts, with the screening records giving chronology only.

**Scene 17. What will be shown.** Auditorium, afternoon.
The group decides how to present the final screening: the passage at its documented festival position, framed as a reconstruction of that screening, with a short note separating the released print from the supplementary material. They decide separately what can responsibly be said about Daniel; owning the letter does not settle permission to publish every private detail. Tobias agrees after Aurel corrects his accusation before the same people who heard it.

**Scene 18. The last changeover.** Auditorium and booth, final evening.
The supplementary passage appears as the real screen, panel by panel. Benedikt recognizes it. Tobias watches from the house. Aurel logs the screening precisely: source, insertion position, supplementary material, destination. Priya receives the conserved material with its distinctions intact. Her count reaches zero. Aurel switches off the lamp. His last act of expertise is a record another person can examine without inheriting his certainty.

## Open decisions resolved by this outline

- 1987 never appears as direct memory, only as attributed reconstruction, until Scene 18.
- The second hand is Daniel's, first seen in Scene 02, matched in Scene 16.
- Priya's count is the structural device.
- The closing screening is shown in panels of the actual screen, with audience reaction.
